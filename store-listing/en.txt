📺 YT Nickname Restorer

📖 Introduction
YT Nickname Restorer is a browser extension designed specifically for YouTube viewers. To solve the issue of YouTube replacing user names with unrecognizable Handles (e.g., @User-xk9lp...), this tool automatically restores these codes to the channel's real display name in Live Chats and Comments—and lets you save your own private custom nicknames and notes for regulars.

We adhere strictly to a "100% Local, Privacy First" policy. Name lookups are sent directly from your browser to YouTube's public channel pages (without your login cookies), and nothing is ever uploaded to the developer or any third-party server.

🚀 Key Features

1. Automatic Nickname Restoration
- Support Range: Live Chat (including chat replay), Comments, and YouTube Studio.
- How it works: When you watch a video or live stream, the extension automatically detects @Handle IDs on the screen and looks up their real channel names in the background.
- Native Style Preservation: With "Dual-Node Replace" on (default), YouTube's original name colors, member badges, and channel owner styles are kept.
- Smart Cache Lifecycle: Names are cached locally for 15 days by default before refreshing in the background, and entries not refreshed for 30 days are cleaned up automatically to save space.

2. Local Custom Nicknames & Notes
- Click any chatter's avatar in Live Chat (including chat replay and YouTube Studio live chat) to open your private notes panel (can be toggled on/off in settings; enabled by default).
- Custom Nickname: Give regulars a memorable alias (up to 40 characters) that replaces their displayed name in Live Chat (preserving member colors; hover to see the original channel name).
- Personal Notes: Add or remove text notes for each viewer (up to 50 notes, 500 characters each). Avatars with notes show a subtle ring—hover over the avatar to preview them.
- Dedicated Management Tab: Switch to the "Nicknames & Notes" tab in Cache Manager to search, edit, delete, export, or import all local records in one place.
- Completely Private: Stored separately in your browser, never uploaded, and kept even if you clear the channel name cache.

3. Smart Tooltip
Hover your mouse over a restored name to see a floating info card:
- Real Name: Displays the full channel name.
- Original Handle: For easy verification of the original @ID.
- Subscriber Count & Badge: Shows the subscriber count, with a colored dot next to the name based on channel size:
  🟢 Cyan: 1 Million+ (1M+)
  🟡 Gold: 100k+
  ⚪ Silver: 10k+
  🟠 Bronze: 1,000+ (1k+)
  🟤 Brown: Rising Channel (500+)
- Expired Notice: Lets you know when an entry is outdated and queued for a background refresh.

4. Flexible Settings (Click Extension Icon)
- Language Switcher: Supports English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, and Japanese.
- Theme Switcher: Choose between Follow System, Light, and Dark mode—applied to the popup, Cache Manager, and chat notes panel.
- Max Name Length: Set the display character limit (5–50, default: 20 chars) to keep chat tidy.
- Click to Copy URL: When enabled, clicking a restored name copies the channel URL.
- Dual-Node Replace: Keeps YouTube's original name colors and member styles (page reload needed after changing).
- Nicknames & Notes Toggle: Turn avatar editing and custom nickname display on or off anytime.
- Fetch Speed Control: Choose between "Normal" and "Slow (Safe Mode)" to match your viewing habits.
- Customizable cache expiration and auto-delete days.

💾 Import & Export: The Ultimate Community Tool
Located in the "Cache Manager", sharing a backup file (.json) lets fan communities build a shared regulars list together.

How to use:
1. Click the extension icon in the top right corner of your browser.
2. Click "📋 Open Cache Manager".
3. Use "Export" to save your collected channel names as a JSON file, or "Import" to merge a file shared by others into your local list.
4. Your custom nicknames and notes are exported and imported separately, from the "Nicknames & Notes" tab. Importing merges them with what you have: notes are combined and nothing is deleted.

🤝 Why use Import?
- Crowd-sourced "Regulars List": Veteran fans can share their accumulated list in Discord or fan groups so newcomers recognize regulars right away.
- Instant Display, No Waiting: Names already in your list appear immediately even in busy live chats.
- Safe Import: Choose "Safe Import (Mark Expired)" to mark imported entries as expired; they display right away and are re-checked in the background when they appear on screen.

🛠️ FAQ & Troubleshooting

Q1: Why do some names have a dotted underline?
A: The entry was imported with "Safe Import" or hasn't been refreshed for longer than the expiration period (15 days by default). It still displays normally and is updated in the background the next time that name appears.

Q2: What if names stop restoring?
A: Looking up many new names in a short time may get temporarily limited by YouTube; the extension slows down automatically. If lookups keep failing, it pauses new lookups and keeps showing names it already has. Wait a few minutes, then open the extension popup and click the "⚠️ Backend Fuse (Fetcher)" indicator to resume. If you keep live streams open for long periods, switch to "Slow (Safe Mode)" in settings. If it keeps happening, YouTube may have changed its pages; please let us know via "🐦 Report Issue" in the popup.

Q3: Will my nicknames, notes, or browsing data be uploaded?
A: Never. The extension only contacts YouTube to look up channel names; your cache, nicknames, and notes stay in your browser. Exported JSON files are saved only to your own computer.

Q4: Can I use it together with the StreamMemo creator extension?
A: Please don't enable both in the same browser profile; both rewrite chat names and would conflict.
