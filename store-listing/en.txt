📺 YT Nickname Restorer

📖 Introduction
YT Nickname Restorer is a browser extension designed specifically for YouTube viewers. To solve the issue of YouTube replacing user names with unrecognizable Handles (e.g., @User-xk9lp...), this tool automatically restores these codes to the channel's real display name in Live Chats and Comments—and lets you save your own private custom nicknames and notes for regulars.

We adhere strictly to a "100% Local, Privacy First" policy. All lookups are sent directly from your browser to YouTube, and nothing is ever uploaded to any third-party server.

🚀 Key Features

1. Automatic Nickname Restoration
- Support Range: Live Chat, Comments, and YouTube Studio.
- How it works: When you watch a video or live stream, the extension automatically detects @Handle IDs on the screen and silently looks up their real channel names in the background.
- Native Style Preservation: Keeps YouTube's original name colors, member badges, and channel owner styles intact without flickering.
- Smart Cache Lifecycle: Names are cached locally for 15 days by default before refreshing automatically in the background, and unused entries are cleaned up after 30 days to save space.

2. Local Custom Nicknames & Notes (New in v1.3.0)
- Click any chatter's avatar in Live Chat to open your private notes panel (can be toggled on/off in settings; enabled by default).
- Custom Nickname: Give regulars a memorable alias that directly replaces their displayed name in chat (preserving member colors; hover to see the original channel name).
- Personal Notes: Add or remove multiple text notes for each viewer. Avatars with notes show a subtle ring—hover over the avatar to preview anytime.
- Dedicated Management Tab: Switch to the "Nicknames & Notes" tab in Cache Manager to search, edit, delete, export, or import all local records in one place.
- Completely Private: Stored separately in your browser, never uploaded, and kept safe even if you clear the channel name cache.

3. Smart Tooltip
Hover your mouse over a restored name to see a floating info card:
- Real Name: Displays the full channel name.
- Original Handle: For easy verification of the original @ID.
- Subscriber Badge: Displays a colored dot based on subscriber count:
  🟢 Cyan: 1 Million+ (1M+)
  🟡 Gold: 100k+
  ⚪ Silver: 10k+
  🟠 Bronze: 1,000+ (1k+)
  🟤 Brown: Rising Channel (500+)
- Expired Notice: Lets you know when an entry is queued for a silent background refresh.

4. Flexible Settings (Click Extension Icon)
- Language Switcher: Supports English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, and Japanese.
- Theme Switcher: Choose between Follow System, Light, and Dark mode—synced across all extension windows and panels.
- Max Name Length: Set the display character limit (default: 20 chars) to keep chat tidy.
- Click to Copy URL: When enabled, clicking a restored name copies the channel URL directly.
- Nicknames & Notes Toggle: Turn avatar editing and custom nickname display on or off anytime.
- Fetch Speed Control: Choose between "Normal" and "Slow (Safe Mode)" to match your viewing habits.
- Customizable cache expiration and auto-cleanup days.

💾 Import & Export: The Ultimate Community Tool
Located in the "Cache Manager", sharing a backup file (.json) lets fan communities build a shared regulars list together.

How to use:
1. Click the extension icon in the top right corner of your browser.
2. Click "📋 Open Cache Manager".
3. Use Export to save your collected channel names as a JSON file, or Import to merge a file shared by others into your local list.

🤝 Why use Import?
- Crowd-sourced "Regulars List": Veteran fans can share their accumulated list in Discord or fan groups so newcomers recognize regulars right away.
- Instant Display, No Waiting: Names already in your list appear immediately even in busy live chats.
- Smart Verification: Imported entries can be marked for background verification so you get instant display while keeping names accurate.

🛠️ FAQ & Troubleshooting

Q1: Why do some names have a dotted underline?
A: This means the entry was imported or hasn't been refreshed for over 15 days. It still displays normally and will update silently the next time that viewer speaks.

Q2: What if names stop restoring temporarily?
A: Fetching too many new names in a short time may trigger a temporary pause from YouTube. Wait about 5 minutes to recover, or switch to "Slow Mode" in settings if you keep live streams open for long periods.

Q3: Will my nicknames, notes, or browsing data be uploaded?
A: Never. This extension operates 100% locally in your browser with no server uploads. Exported JSON files are saved only to your own computer.
